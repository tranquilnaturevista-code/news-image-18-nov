A front page summing up the world's news of Wednesday 18 November 2026 in one image, built for [make.fun's news-image-20261118 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261118).

`main.py` copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`. The image is a newspaper front page: Trump and Xi meet in Shenzhen as the APEC summit opens, US jets strike a missile site on Qeshm Island as Iran answers in Bahrain and Kuwait, Ukraine hits the Crimean Bridge, Nvidia posts record $115.8B earnings, and Peltola flips Alaska to give Democrats a 56-44 Senate majority.
